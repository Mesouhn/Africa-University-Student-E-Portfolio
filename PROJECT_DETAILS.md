## Project Abstract



The Africa University Student E-Portfolio Management System is a web-based platform designed to help students record, manage and showcase their academic and extracurricular achievements. Students can maintain their portfolios, submit achievements for verification and track the status of their submissions. Authorized staff members can review submissions and approve or reject them with reasons. The Portal Administrator manages staff accounts and oversees achievement records. The system uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Netlify Functions to support secure authentication, role-based access and backend operations. Its purpose is to provide a centralized and organized way of managing student achievements while improving verification, accountability and access to student portfolio information.
